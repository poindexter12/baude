Drop removes the tree; `NotFound` is fine; on `PermissionDenied` it makes the tree owner-writable and retries once; any remaining error is printed with `eprintln!`, never a panic.
- `pub struct RuntimeReaper`: `for_identity(&ProcessIdentity)` and `for_pty(&crate::pty::Pty)`. Drop sends SIGKILL to `-process_group`, ignoring `ESRCH` and `EPERM`; then, only when `session == pid as i32`, enumerates `ps -eo pid=` and SIGKILLs every pid whose `libc::getsid` equals the session; then polls up to 5 s until no live member remains, where `ps -o stat=` starting with `Z` counts as gone. It never calls `waitpid`: the `Pty` owns its child. It never panics; a survivor is printed with `eprintln!`.
- Both are compiled under `cfg(any(test, feature = "test-support"))` with the rest of the module.
