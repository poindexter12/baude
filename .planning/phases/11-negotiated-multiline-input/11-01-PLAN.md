## Planner assumptions (mechanisms each requirement relies on — flagged)

- TKEY-02 relies on **event re-encoding**: baude consumes crossterm KeyEvents and
  re-encodes to legacy bytes via encode_key (app.rs:3923-3964, keys.rs:6-91), so
  outer kitty mode cannot change any key's child bytes except the one arm this
  plan rewrites.
- TKEY-03 relies on **probe-or-legacy**: on a legacy outer, Shift+Enter arrives as
  a plain Enter KeyEvent (no SHIFT bit) — the legacy path is automatic; nothing
  is synthesized.
- TKEY-04 relies on the **single restore point**: restore_terminal() (main.rs:98-106)
  already serves the panic hook (386-390) and normal/error exit (409-414).
- TKEY-05 relies on the **bounded probe** (crossterm-internal hardcoded 2000 ms —
  NOT tunable, per RESEARCH Pitfall 1; do not add a timeout parameter) plus
  **dual verification** (outer probe AND observed child push, completed in 11-04).
