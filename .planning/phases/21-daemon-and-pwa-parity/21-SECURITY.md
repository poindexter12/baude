# Phase 21 — Security

> Per-phase security contract: threat register, accepted risks, and audit trail.

Audited by gsd-security-auditor at `149380b`, which includes the five review-fix commits. Register authored at plan time across the 11 plans, 80 threat IDs: 64 mitigate, 15 accept, 1 transfer. The auditor found evidence in code for 62 of the 64 mitigations. The other two, T-21-92 and T-21-93, are procedural controls of the 21-11 live fixture daemon; they could not be verified after the fact and were accepted by the owner on 2026-11-13, not verified.

On 2026-11-15 plan 22.1-11 re-pointed every row that cited the removal code at the WR-06 fix and moved T-21-02 from accept to mitigate; the counts above are the audit's, and the audit trail records the change. Line anchors in rows not marked as re-pointed are still those of `149380b`, which is not an ancestor of the Phase 22.1 branch.

Notes from the audit:

- Review finding WR-06 was fixed in Phase 22.1, plans 22.1-08 to 22.1-11. A removal now takes the manager lock only to begin and to finish, and stops runtimes and deletes worktrees between the two with the lock released. T-21-14, T-21-28 and T-21-53 now rest on the repository or folder reservation the removal holds across that window, not on the mutex. T-21-02 moved from accept to mitigate and AR-21-01 is retired, both on 2026-11-15.
- T-21-71: the plan named the test `removal_oversize_json_body_is_refused`; after review fix WR-04 it is `removal_oversize_json_body_on_an_execute_200_is_an_unknown_outcome`. The 1 MiB cap is unchanged.
- T-21-53: the planned early-send mutant gate is not a standing script or CI step; closed on the single publish call and the four silence tests.
- T-21-65: closed on the code control and its test; the human tap check was not performed.
