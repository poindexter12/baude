<objective>
Publish removals to every connected client. `Manager` owns a `tokio::sync::broadcast` channel of `DaemonEvent`; `remove_rows` sends `RowsRemoved(report)` after core's save stage returns `Ok`, inside the caller's lock (D-07); `GET /events` bridges a receiver to an SSE stream with event name `rows_removed` and the report as data (D-05, D-06). The two per-session streams stay as they are. Discretion recorded: capacity 64 and skip-on-lag, because the PWA's 3 s poll and the TUI's poll reconcile anything a lagged client missed.

Purpose: a PWA or TUI drops the removed rows the instant the daemon has durably dropped them, without a refetch.
Output: channel, route, and tests that read real SSE frames from the router body.
</objective>
