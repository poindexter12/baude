# Phase 21 — Plan Outline (replan cycle 3)

Six plans existed and were byte-identical across two review cycles; `21-REVIEWS.md` carries
10 HIGH and 41 actionable findings, none incorporated. This outline is **not** a reproduction
of that six-plan split. Four of the ten HIGHs are work no current plan owns at all
(standalone admission, client snapshot arbitration, key→name resolution, `ui.rs` custody), and
two cross-plan MEDIUMs are structural (the phase-terminal gate sits inside a plan that shares a
wave with the one most likely to break the build). Those findings force the split below.

## What changed from the six-plan split, and why

- **21-02 shed two thirds of its scope.** It carried `/rows` + `checkout_key` + three row kinds + suppression admission in three tasks while holding two unrelated HIGHs. Row-kind breadth became **21-09**; standalone admission and suppression became **21-10**, because HIGH #4 is real `manager.rs` subsystem work (a standalone runtime registry, restore, non-fabricating admission), not a test fixture.
- **21-05 and 21-06 each split in two.** The client HIGHs added a pure escaping/arbitration layer and a name-resolution path; **21-07** (pure module) and **21-08** (remote client) now hold the testable seams, leaving **21-05** and **21-06** as controller/wiring plans at 2-3 tasks each. 21-06 finally owns `ui.rs`.
- **21-11 is new**, and the only plan added purely for a structural finding.
- **IDs 21-01, 21-02, 21-03, 21-04, 21-05, 21-06 survive** with their identities intact; waves re-sequence because `bauded/src/manager.rs` + `bauded/src/api.rs` ownership forces the daemon plans serial. New work appends as 21-07 … 21-11.
- **Parallelism was recovered where files are genuinely disjoint**, not where a reviewer asked. Wave 3 runs 21-09 (daemon) alongside 21-07 (`bauded/web/*`); wave 7 runs 21-05 (`bauded/web/*`, `bauded/src/web.rs`) alongside 21-08 (`baude/src/remote.rs`).

### One review suggestion rejected, with reason

The claude lane recommends splitting the SSE plan so its channel task runs in wave 2 to shorten the critical path. **Rejected:** that task modifies `bauded/src/manager.rs` and `bauded/src/api.rs`, which 21-02 and 21-09 also own, and GSD requires zero `files_modified` overlap within a wave. The dependency reading is correct; the parallelism is not available. Recorded in the 21-04 dispositions ledger rather than silently dropped.
