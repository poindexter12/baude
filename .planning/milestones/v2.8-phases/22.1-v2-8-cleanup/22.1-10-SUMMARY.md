# Phase 22.1 Plan 10: WR-06, the removal releases the manager lock Summary

`POST /removals` no longer holds the manager mutex across the kill and the delete. It takes the lock twice: once to recheck, reserve and prepare, and once to commit, save and publish. The slow stage runs between them with no lock, on the same blocking thread, inside one `catch_unwind`. Other requests are served during that window. A removal still commits all or nothing, still refuses a second removal of the same rows, and still publishes only after its save returned. Each guarantee is held by a named test, and each red was shown failing for its named reason before its fix.

## Lock scopes, by function

| Stage | Function | Lock | What runs |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `Manager::begin_removal` | held | persistence-blocked check, repository or folder reservation, fresh preview recheck with 409 on drift, merge of the doomed rows' live metadata into the stored rows, `collect_row_runtimes`, `removal::prepare`; a prepare error puts the handles back |
| 2 | `RemovalInFlight::stop_and_delete` | none | one `catch_unwind(AssertUnwindSafe(..))` closure: the `#[cfg(test)]` pause first, then `removal::stop_and_delete`. The handles stay owned by the in-flight value |
| 3 | `Manager::finish_removal` | held | an error or a panic from stage 2 puts every handle back and returns 500. On success the survivor and standalone overrides are taken now, the save closure is built, and `removal::commit` runs. A commit error puts every handle back, sets the persistence flags on a persist failure, and returns. On success: registry cleanup, permission wake-ups, persistence flags, then the publish |

- `execute_removal` captures the test redirects, then runs `run_removal` in one `tokio::task::spawn_blocking` closure that installs them first. A `JoinError` answers 500 `removal task failed: ...`. No `MutexGuard` crosses an await. The busy bodies are built under the lock in `removal_refusal`.
- The reservation guards are fields of `RemovalInFlight`. They are bound in `finish_removal` and drop when it returns, after the publish.
- **The only non-test publish** is `self.publish(DaemonEvent::RowsRemoved(report.clone()))` in `finish_removal`, on the committed path. The other caller is the `#[cfg(test)]` `publish_for_test`. `remove_rows` is deleted.
- The comment above `preview_removal` and the one above the publish were rewritten. They no longer say the caller holds the mutex for the whole removal.

### Poll carry-over from 22.1-09

`poll_defers_an_early_resume_reconcile_while_its_repository_is_busy` holds the real repository reservation across a `poll()` that meets an early targeted-resume exit. The poll logs `failed to reconcile early targeted resume: repository busy: <repo>`. It does not panic, and it keeps both the session and its `runtime_checkouts` entry. Once the hold drops, the next `poll()` reconciles the row and saves `resume_id: None`. The doomed sessions themselves cannot meet this path, because they are out of the registry for the whole window.

## Open Items

If a removal's slow stage holds the repository past that window, the poll's deferred reconcile is never retried. The session stays an ordinary exited session instead of being reconciled through `Manager::remove`.
